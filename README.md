# CatSketch

**A cozy place to sketch ideas together.**

CatSketch brings a shared whiteboard, conversation, and a little cat personality into one workspace. It is made for brainstorming with friends, explaining an idea, planning a project, tutoring, and drawing together without a complicated setup.

## Start with an idea

Open a canvas and start drawing immediately—no account needed. Private sketches stay in your browser, ready to continue later.

When you want company, sign in or create an account and share your canvas. Your drawing carries into the shared space, and you get an invitation link. Friends can join with just a name.

## Create together

- Draw freely, add text, connect ideas with arrows, and organize diagrams.
- See everyone's edits and cursors as they happen.
- Talk through ideas with audio calls and send messages in the space's chat.
- Choose a blank canvas or start from a useful template.
- Export a sketch as an image when you want to take it elsewhere.
- Enjoy colorful cat avatars and playful cats across the website.

## A home for your spaces

After signing in, your dashboard brings together your shared spaces, their details, and private drafts from the current browser. Your saved name and cat avatar follow you into your spaces.

Your profile page gives you access to your account information and account deletion. Owners can delete a space, including its whiteboard, messages, and related data.
